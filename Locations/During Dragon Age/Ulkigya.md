Ulkige of race and -ya of Baya for god or greater than/enhanced

Ulkigya is inhabited by a people who are often blessed by [[Levya, Goddess of Fortune]]. Deep within the heart of Ulkigya is a place known as the [[Slums of Chaos]] which is full of a dense and thick twist of [[Chaos]] due to Levya being present within. It is heavily guarded by blessed super soldiers of Levya’s.
