The first to receive the title of [[God-Slayers|God-Slayer]].

## Early Life
Born in a town that was terrorized by [[Yeraz, God of Sin]]. The people of the town were forced into worshipping Yeraz, despite not knowing that he was the cause for all their troubles. Kinnuah eventually figured out what was happening and told his friend, [[Perval]], that he was going to figure out a way to kill Yeraz. Unfortunately, Yeraz heard this and used his [[Oath#Pact|pact]] to remove Kinnuah’s legs. Perval saw this and then had reason to believe in Kinnuah and so they ran away with Kinnuah. Kinnuah then began to train their arms and using a sword due to not having legs to learn martial arts. A year later, Yeraz’s enforcement expired and Kinnuah regained his legs, but his brain was still not fully used to using them again and so Kinnuah learned very slowly to use them and spent a lot of his energy collecting intel on Yeraz and other [[Gods]].

## The Journey
Once Kinnuah had learned of many of the wrongdoings from other Gods, Kinnuah set out on a journey to go around and kill as many Gods as he could, with the help of Perval.
