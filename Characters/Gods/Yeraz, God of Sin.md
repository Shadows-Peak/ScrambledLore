Yeraz has been encoded with the [[Oath#Pact|Pact of Morality]] by [[Legyre, The Storyteller|Legyre]]. He has been around since at least a bit after [[The Ring Wars]].

## Pact of Morality
Yeraz must only use his powers of [[Chaos Magic]] to enforce the principles of morality. This concept of morality is upheld by the formed conscience of the nearest ten thousand non-[[Chaos]] users to Yeraz’s physical body. This formed conscience exists only for the sake of the pact, and it is not aware of its existence nor are the people that contribute to it. The limits of “enforcement” on the principles of morality are as follows:
* All enforcements must be neutral in sum by the end of each year (10 reprehensible actions could be taken and then 10 saintly actions, and or infinite neutral actions)
* An enforcement is only allowed to (at most) exceed the formed conscience’s perception of a matter in terms of its placement on a moral compass by 10% whatever the formed conscience would score an action in that direction (evil v. good)
* Any singular enforcement cannot last for longer than a year at most. Long-lasting enforcements do not affect the perception of future enforcements and their validity (If you used an enforcement to bring drought to a farm, you could also kill their sheep in a separate enforcement to that farm as long as it’s deemed a fine enforcement by the formed conscience when ignoring already active enforcements) **This is the loophole that allows Yeraz to commit terrible acts**

In terms of the formed conscience, Yeraz can use the Pact of Morality to interact with it in the following ways:
* Probe the overall standing of how the formed conscience feels over a matter, as well as receive a score  from them in terms of placement on a moral compass and the direction of morality given
* Compare the current formed conscience to the last 10 years’ formed consciences that Yeraz has had to determine whether or not the current one is deemed as more evil or more good in terms of the previous formed conscience’s understandings and beliefs
* Gauge the formed conscience’s feelings over any currently active enforcement, or action that would take place upon Yeraz

Yeraz is allowed to protect himself with their Chaos Magic given they don’t violate their own idea of morality with whatever action they take. The idea of “protecting himself” is rigid in the fact that it is only allowed once something is done to Yeraz deemed reprehensible by the formed conscience, and this matter of using their Chaos Magic is only allowed over the party that inflicted this original reprehensible action. Failure to uphold this, or the formed conscience’s beliefs and the other rulings, will result in an [[Gods#Erosion of Will|Erosion of Will]].

## Manipulation
Yeraz has come to be known as the God of Sin, not of Morality, as he keeps hidden how exactly his Pact of Morality works, and instead allows people to believe that he simply enforces the sins of others and commits sins against people. Unbeknownst to most, Yeraz must spend a portion of the year committing as many saintly acts as possible so as to avoid Erosion of Will.

Yeraz’s main ploy has been getting even the tiniest negative edge over an area, and taking advantage of the fact that that isn’t counted into the formed conscience’s interpretation of something. For example, if Yeraz made conditions so bad somewhere that people had to turn to crime, the formed conscience would only see the fact that people are committing crime, not the fact that it was previous enforcements from Yeraz that forced this.
