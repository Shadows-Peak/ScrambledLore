Not an actual faction at any given point in time, but instead a title given out to those who are of mortal compositions deemed inferior and or lesser than to the [[Gods]] and yet is still capable of felling one and has done so at least once before with the intent of removal of a sect of gods or more. You could likely also gain this title if you felled a prominent [[Chaos Dragons|Chaos Dragon]] such as [[Vokzan, the Prophet|Vokzan]], though Vokzan in particular would likely be too impartial or beneficial a force to mortals to warrant any disdain towards them that would properly motivate the title of God-Slayer.
